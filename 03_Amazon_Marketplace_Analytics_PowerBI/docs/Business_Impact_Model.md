# Business Impact Model

## Concentration Risk
Top-10 GMV: **$3,575,099.21**

If Top-10 GMV declines with no offset:
- 5% decline → **$178,754.96** GMV at risk
- 10% decline → **$357,509.92** GMV at risk
- 20% decline → **$715,019.84** GMV at risk

## Long-Tail Growth
Long-tail baseline GMV: **$4,630,963.57**
- 3% uplift → **$138,928.91**
- 5% uplift → **$231,548.18**
- 10% uplift → **$463,096.36**

These are scenario estimates. They assume no mix shift, cannibalization, pricing change, or offsetting seller behavior.
