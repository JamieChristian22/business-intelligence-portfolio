# Power BI Report Design

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
Budget, Actual, Variance $, Variance %, Revenue, Gross Margin, Operating Margin; trend and department variance.

## Page 2 — Department Performance
Department budget, actual, variance, variance %, trend, account mix, conditional formatting.

## Page 3 — Account Drivers
Payroll, Software, Travel, Ads, Facilities, Contractors, Cloud; contribution to total overspend.

## Page 4 — Profitability
Revenue, COGS, Gross Profit, Gross Margin, Opex, Operating Profit, Operating Margin over time.

## Page 5 — Forecast & Outlook
3-month rolling revenue/expense run rates, annualized outlook, monthly actual vs budget, variance trend.

## Page 6 — Management Actions
Overspend pool, savings scenarios, repeated variance flags, departments/accounts requiring intervention.

Use synced Date, Department, and Account slicers plus drillthrough and tooltip pages.
