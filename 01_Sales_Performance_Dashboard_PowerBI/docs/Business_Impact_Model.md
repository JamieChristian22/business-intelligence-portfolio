# Business Impact Model

## Baseline
- Leads: 383,804
- Deals won: 26,672
- Conversion: 6.9494%
- Revenue: $151,027,624.12
- Average revenue per win: $5,662.40

## Formula
`Incremental Revenue = Current Deals Won × Relative Lift % × Average Revenue per Won Deal`

| Relative Lift | Incremental Wins | Estimated Incremental Revenue |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1% | 267 | $1,510,276.24 |
| 3% | 800 | $4,530,828.72 |
| 5% | 1,334 | $7,551,381.21 |

These are scenario estimates, not guaranteed outcomes. Lead volume and average deal economics are held constant.
