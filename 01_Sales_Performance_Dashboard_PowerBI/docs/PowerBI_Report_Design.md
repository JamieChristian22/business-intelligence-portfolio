# Power BI Report Design

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
Revenue, Profit, Margin, Conversion, Average Deal Size; monthly trend; revenue by region; channel conversion; slicers.

## Page 2 — Regional Performance
Revenue rank/share, profit margin, conversion, average deal size, trend, below-average flags.

## Page 3 — Channel & Funnel
Leads, wins, conversion, revenue per lead, average deal size, channel contribution and drillthrough.

## Page 4 — Product Performance
Revenue, profit, conversion, rank, contribution and time trend.

## Page 5 — Diagnostic Detail
Region × Channel × Product matrix with conditional formatting and tooltip context.

## Modeling Standards
Single-direction dimension-to-fact relationships; hide keys; mark date table; sort MonthName by Month; use explicit measures; sync slicers.
