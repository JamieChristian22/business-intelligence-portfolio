# Business Impact Model

## Purpose
Quantify the effect of improving Mobile conversion without inventing new traffic or order-value assumptions.

## Mobile Baseline
- Sessions: 1,839,613
- Orders: 40,845
- Conversion rate: 2.2203%
- Average order value: $79.85

## Formula
`Incremental Orders = Current Mobile Orders × Relative Lift`

`Incremental Revenue = Incremental Orders × Current Mobile AOV`

| Relative Lift | Incremental Orders | Incremental Revenue | New Conversion Rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1% | 408.4 | $32,613.79 | 2.2425% |
| 3% | 1,225.3 | $97,841.38 | 2.2869% |
| 5% | 2,042.2 | $163,068.97 | 2.3313% |
| 10% | 4,084.5 | $326,137.93 | 2.4423% |

## Assumptions
- Mobile sessions remain constant.
- Mobile AOV remains constant.
- Lift represents relative improvement in orders, not percentage-point addition to conversion.
- Returns, discounts, margin effects, and acquisition-cost changes are not modeled.
- Scenario outputs are directional estimates and should be validated through experimentation.
