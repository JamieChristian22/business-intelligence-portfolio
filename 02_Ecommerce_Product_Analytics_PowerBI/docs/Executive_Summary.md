# Executive Summary

## Objective
Identify the strongest ecommerce growth levers across devices and traffic sources using reproducible KPI analysis.

## Baseline Performance
- Sessions: **3,812,702**
- Orders: **94,901**
- Revenue: **$7,650,421.07**
- Conversion rate: **2.49%**
- AOV: **$80.61**
- Return rate: **5.90%**
- Weighted bounce rate: **42.02%**

## Executive Findings
Mobile controls the largest session base but converts at **2.22%**, below Desktop and Tablet. Email has the highest AOV at **$85.06**. Paid Search is the largest traffic and order source.

## Recommended Decision
Prioritize mobile conversion work, preserve Email customer quality, and optimize Paid Search at landing-page/source-segment level. Track conversion, AOV, revenue/session, bounce, and returns together so growth is not judged by traffic volume alone.
