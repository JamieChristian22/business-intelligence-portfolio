# Retention Methodology

Event tracking begins **2025-02-24** and ends **2026-02-24**.

To avoid left-censoring:
- activation includes only customers who signed up on/after tracking began and have a complete seven-day observation window;
- D30 retention includes only signup cohorts with at least 36 days of follow-up;
- D60 and D90 follow the same eligibility principle.

Retention uses seven-day milestone windows rather than one exact day because product usage is not necessarily daily.

This methodology is intentionally conservative and reproducible.
