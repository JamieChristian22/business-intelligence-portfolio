# Tableau Dashboard Design

## Dashboard 1 — Executive Product Health
MRR, ARR, Active Customers, Activation, D30 Retention, DAU/MAU, recorded churn; monthly active-user trend and MRR by plan.

## Dashboard 2 — Activation & Onboarding
Activation rate by signup cohort and plan; first-seven-day sessions and feature-event distribution; activated vs non-activated retention.

## Dashboard 3 — Retention & Cohorts
D7/D30/D60/D90 cards; cohort heatmap; cohort-size context; plan filters.

## Dashboard 4 — Engagement
DAU, MAU, stickiness, sessions/user, feature events/user, active days; power-user vs at-risk segmentation.

## Dashboard 5 — Customer Health
User health scorecard, plan value, engagement, support burden, risk segment; drill-through to customer detail.

## Dashboard 6 — Revenue & Churn
MRR by plan, churned MRR, churn-reduction scenarios, ARPU and simple LTV proxy.

Filters: Signup Cohort, Plan, Date, Health Segment. Use dashboard actions for customer and cohort drill-through.
