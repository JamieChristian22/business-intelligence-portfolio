# Churn Impact Model

## Recorded Baseline
Recorded churned customers: **32**  
Recorded churned MRR: **$1,788.00**

## Formula
`MRR Retained = Recorded Churned MRR × Relative Churn Reduction`

`Annualized Revenue Retained = MRR Retained × 12`

This model answers a narrow question: how much recurring revenue represented by observed churn could be retained if a fraction of recorded churn were prevented?

It does not assume expansion, price changes, new-logo growth, or CAC effects.
