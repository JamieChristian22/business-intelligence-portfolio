# Metric Definitions

## Active Customer
SignupDate <= 2026-02-24 and ChurnDate is blank or later than 2026-02-24.

## MRR
Sum of current monthly plan price for active customers.

## ARR Run Rate
MRR × 12. This is a run-rate metric, not GAAP revenue.

## Activation
At least 3 sessions AND 5 feature events within lifecycle days 0–6.

## Dn Retention
At least one activity row during lifecycle days n through n+6.

## DAU
Distinct active customers with a recorded event row on a date.

## MAU
Distinct active customers with a recorded event row in the trailing 30-day period.

## Stickiness
Average trailing-30-day DAU / trailing-30-day MAU.

## ARPU
MRR / active customers.

## Simple LTV Proxy
ARPU / estimated monthly logo churn. This is a simplified directional metric and excludes gross margin and acquisition cost.
